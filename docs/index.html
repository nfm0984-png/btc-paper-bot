How the bot trades

This is a long-only Bitcoin paper bot. It never shorts, never uses leverage, and never places a real order. It looks at closed 1-hour BTC/USD candles and waits for a confirmed uptrend with healthy momentum before buying.

  1. Market. BTC/USD, 1-hour candles from Coinbase (Kraken as fallback). Signals use only closed hours, not the candle still forming.
  2. Indicators. Fast EMA 12, slow EMA 26, trend EMA 50, RSI 14, and the MACD histogram (12, 26, 9).
  3. Buy (go long) when flat if all of these are true on a closed hour: EMA 12 is above EMA 26, the close is above EMA 50, the MACD histogram is positive, and RSI is between 52 and 72 (enough momentum, but not a blow-off top).
  4. Sell back to cash if any of these happen: EMA 12 crosses back below EMA 26 on a closed hour; price drops 3.0% below the entry fill (hard stop); or, once the trade is up 2.0%, a trailing stop 2.5% below the highest price since entry is hit.
  5. Position size. 95% of available paper cash, leaving a 5% cash buffer.
  6. Costs. Every buy and every sell is charged 0.10% fee + 0.05% slippage (0.15% per side), so a round trip costs about 0.30% before the market even moves.
  7. Starting book. $1,000 in simulated cash. Live paper trades start from the first bot run and are kept separate from the historical backtest.
Paper trading only. Not financial advice, and not an offer to buy or sell anything. Past backtests do not promise future results.
