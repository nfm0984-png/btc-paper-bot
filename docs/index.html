How the bot trades

This is a paper bot for Kalshi’s KXBTC15M market — “BTC price up in the next 15 mins?” It never places a real order. Yes wins if the CF Benchmarks BRTI average in the last 60 seconds of the window is at or above the strike. No wins if it finishes below.

  1. Head-to-head $100 race. Two paper books start at the same moment with $100 each so you can watch which style actually makes more money. Both use flat $4–$8 per trade ($4 / $6 / $8 by signal strength, no 2× press), plus real Kalshi taker fees. Every ticket’s total cost (including fees) stays between $4 and $8.
  2. Version 1.2 · Chart-Confirmed Active (Race 2, from Nov 27) still trades more often than Version 2, but fixes Race 1’s timing problems. It buys the side BTC is on (Yes above the strike, No below) when BTC is at least $30 from the strike, the ask is 35¢–65¢, and 2+ minutes remain; it scores the 1m EMA 9/21/50, MACD, RSI and the 5-minute trend for conviction; the 15-minute trend must not point against it; and the spot-derived fair value must be at least the ask. It takes at most one ticket per window and never buys the opposite side of a ticket it holds. It holds to official settlement (every early exit tested lost unseen dollars for V1). The hourly learner keeps tuning these numbers inside the same method. Race 1’s Version 1 (Active Multi-Trade, up to 3 tickets per window with flips) is archived on the Trades tab.
  3. Version 2.2 · Strict + Fair-Value Edge follows the learned strategy in strategy_config.json: clean 1m trend (EMA 9/21, EMA 50, MACD, RSI band), the 5-minute trend agrees, BTC $40+ from the strike, a 35¢–75¢ ask, and now spot fair value at or above the ask. At most one trade per window. It sells early at the live Kalshi bid only on a confirmed reversal (BTC back through the strike and the 1m EMA + MACD flipped against it); otherwise it holds to settlement.
  4. Buy Yes (Version 2) when the 1-minute trend is cleanly bullish (EMA 9 above 21, price above EMA 50, MACD histogram positive, RSI in its bullish band), the learned higher-timeframe chart agrees (currently the 5-minute trend), BTC is far enough from the strike, the Yes ask is inside the learned band, and enough time remains. A spot-fair probability from realized volatility is shown on every check and can be used as an edge gate if the hourly learner promotes one.
  5. Buy No (Version 2) is the bearish mirror.
  6. Wait (Version 2) when those conditions conflict, the EMAs are flat, the window is almost over, Kalshi’s price is already too expensive, or (if enabled) the ask is rich vs spot-fair value or the spread is too wide.
  7. Exits. On Nov 27 every exit was re-tested on the walk-forward split, priced at the Kalshi bid: a reversal exit (BTC back through the strike plus a 1m EMA/MACD flip), take-profit at 90¢+ on a momentum stall, and take-profit at 95¢+. For Version 1.2 every early exit lost unseen dollars, so it holds to settlement. For Version 2.2 the reversal exit added unseen dollars without a worse drawdown, so it is on; the take-profit rules stay off. Early sells show as TP or SL / EARLY EXIT in Recent Checks & Trades.
  8. Market & Chart Reader. Each 5-minute tick reads Coinbase 1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute, and 1-hour BTC candles (Kraken fallback), plus Coinbase/Kraken order-book and taker-flow and the live Kalshi KXBTC15M book. The Live tab shows the snapshot; the Learning tab shows which chart and exit rule won on unseen windows.
  9. Sizing. Flat $4–$8 per trade (no press). A normal qualifying signal buys a $4 ticket. A strong signal (clearer EMA / MACD / RSI alignment and more distance from the strike) buys $6. A top-conviction signal buys $8. Every ticket’s total cost, including the Kalshi taker fee, stays between $4 and $8. If cash is under $4 the book skips the trade. The $100 race books, the archived 1-hour $40 run, and the ongoing $100 book each use the same flat sizing. Historical 2× PRESS badges on already-settled tickets stay in the log for accuracy.
  10. Fees. Each race book starts at $100 (earlier $40 race books were lifted to $100 by adding cash, so past trades and P&L stayed the same). The ongoing book starts at $100. The completed 10:59–11:59 AM PT 1-hour $40 run is archived on the Trades tab. Every ticket uses Kalshi’s real taker fee: ceil(0.07 × contracts × price × (1 − price)).
  11. Learns every hour. A GitHub Action pulls newly settled windows, walk-forward tests new parameters on unseen hours (train on the older ~70%, validate on the newest ~30%), and only promotes a change when unseen validation dollar P&L is strictly higher than the live config by at least $1.50, overall P&L is positive, drawdown is acceptable, and a 3-hour cooldown has passed — never just because profit factor rose. That run is free and does not use Grok Bot.
Paper trading only. Not financial advice. A backtest is not a promise of future results. Kalshi settlement uses CF Benchmarks BRTI, which can differ slightly from Coinbase’s last price.
